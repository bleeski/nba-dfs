**Date:** 2026-10-13. **Reviewer:** an independent critic. ---

## 1. Verdict

The safety half of the design is strong: the validator as the only writer, the fallback file before any LLM step, lock-order planning and the completeness check all directly fix how the prior engines lost entries. The two goals are not yet right: at the user's own volume the modeled washout probability is about zero, so the insurance budget does nothing and the hunch test passes automatically, and the field model carries our private model into the simulated opponents while hiding public news from them. Opening week is also at risk, because the late-news path does not reliably fit in 30 minutes with free news sources and the user's manual steps, and the Phase 1 schedule tries to build the whole model stack in one day with a Claude allowance that the build week will also use up.

## 3. Requirements from the brief that are not yet met

1. **"Measure what it can do. Set runtime budgets"** for the full build and the late-news re-run. Every runtime in §4, §5, §6 and §8 is TODO-R9.
2. **"My hunch ... Test it."** No result yet (TODO-R9). The planned test is model-internal and cannot fail at the user's volume (ranks 1 and 3).
3. **"Give me one control for choosing where on that tradeoff to sit."** The control exists, but it moves nothing when modeled P(W) is about zero (rank 1). D3, its default, is TODO-R9.
4. **"Decide whether and how the objective penalizes duplication."** D2 is proposed, but as written it breaks the selection guarantee and depends on an unfitted setting (rank 8).
5. **"Free MILP and constraint solvers ... choose on merits."** TODO-R2. The raw bench exists, but R2's results sections are placeholders.
6. **"Verify each source works today from this machine."** Not yet verified: The Odds API (no key), DFF projections (empty in preseason), the injury PDF (archived reports only), and the NBA `Status` and `Starting` columns.
7. **"Confirm from real DraftKings results ... whether the Captain 1.5x applies to bonuses."** Still SUPPORTED (a 2020 third-party copy), pending the first 2026 Showdown standings. **"Confirm against the live rules pages"** is also open (R6 open question 7).
8. **"If you recommend reusing a component ... justify it in a decision record."** The reuse record, ADR-018, is cited but missing.
9. **"One short decision record per significant choice."** Six ADRs cover a design with at least fifteen significant choices (rank 25).
10. **"Size the research and QA steps so a full night ... fits comfortably."** Still a BET. The weekly window is not sized, and QA is never measured before go-live (ranks 6 and 23).
11. **Problem 1, late news "30 minutes before tip".** Not demonstrated, and contradicted by R4's 5 to 20 minute news lag (rank 2). The Phase 1 late-swap path is "quick swap" only, and post-lock `/news` is undefined (rank 19).
12. **Problem 5 and 6, "how fast does it react when news creates new chalk".** The field ignores public news that reaches us through the LLM (rank 4).
13. **Problem 7, "find a free way to get what's missing".** Before a contest, field size is known only when its name has a prize pool. Payouts depend on optional pastes, and the entry-history table is deferred (rank 11).
14. **Problem 13, rookies.** The named prior (draft slot, age, height) has no permissive source (rank 12).
15. **Problem 16, "a slate brief I can read in two minutes".** The design lists the brief's contents but includes no sample. Add a one-page mock built from the rehearsal data.
16. **"When you say something is verified, show what you ran and what came back."** This is broken for R2's formulation and portfolio claims and for the NBA `Status` and `Starting` columns (ranks 21 and 25).
17. **"Push back the way a senior PM would."** There is no pushback on raising volume 50 to 100x before the model is proven (rank 14).

## 6. Supporting walkthroughs

### 6.1 Late news, minute by minute (my estimates, using the design's own step times)

The night: main Classic first tip 6:00 pm CT (games at 6:00, 6:30, 7:00 and 9:00), plus Showdowns on the 6:00 and 9:00 games.

| Time (CT) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5:35 (T-25) | A questionable star in the 6:00 game is ruled out. A reporter posts it; a Bluesky mirror may carry it within a minute or two, but the watcher does not poll Bluesky (§8). |
| 5:45 (T-15) | The team's update appears in the next 15-minute injury PDF, if it was filed in time; otherwise at 6:00. The watcher polls every 15 minutes and alerts at about 5:46. |
| 5:46 to 5:50 | The user re-exports four files (Classic main and the 6:00 Showdown, salary and entries each), at about 1 minute each. |
| 5:50 to 5:51 | The user opens a fresh Claude session (§7 says `/news` starts fresh) and types `/news`. Preflight runs. Contingency swaps are written within seconds, but they are single-slot (rank 13). |
| 5:51 to 5:54 | Narrow re-run (TODO-R9, 3-minute target). The LLM step is skipped. |
| 5:54 to 5:58 | The user uploads 2 files and reads back the counts (§8 budgets 1 to 2 minutes per file). |
| 5:58 to 6:00 | The user re-downloads 2 entries files and runs `/status`. Any row DraftKings rejected is found at or after lock. The design's 18-minute buffer is gone. |

**Second event at 5:45:** a starter in the 6:30 game is ruled out. The user is mid-upload. If they start `/news` from the 5:50 exports before event A's file lands, event B's Classic file carries pre-A lineups for shared entries and can revert A's swaps (rank 9). Done sequentially, B is handled after 6:00 by `/swap`, which in Phase 1 only replaces OUT players. That works, but B's new chalk is then never reflected in the field.

**With the fixes in ranks 2 and 13**, at 5:46 the user uploads the prebuilt "IF <star> OUT" files for the two slates (about 3 minutes), re-downloads and checks by about 5:52, and has 8 minutes of buffer.

### 6.2 Manual steps on a typical night (4 slates: Classic main plus 3 Showdowns, about 10 contests)

| Stage | Actions | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Afternoon setup | 8 downloads (entries and salary per slate); payout pastes for new contests (about 10 x 45 s in week 1) | 6, plus up to 7.5 in week 1 |
| Build and upload | `/night`; read the brief; 4 uploads with read-back; 4 re-downloads; `/status` | 10 to 12 |
| Optional fallback upload | 4 uploads | 4 |
| Lock waves (about 3; re-export required each time per §8; news in 2) | 2 to 4 re-exports per wave; `/news`, upload, re-download and `/status` when there is news | 12 to 18 |
| Post-lock swap (1 wave) | Re-download, `/swap`, upload, re-download, `/status` | 4 to 5 |
| Next morning | 10 standings zips (about 20 s each) and `/results` | 4 to 5 |
| **Total** | **About 30 to 40 file actions** | **About 40 to 55 a night (more in week 1)**, against the design's stated "about 10 + about 5 per news wave + about 3" |

Every file action is a chance to drop the wrong file in the inbox or upload the wrong version. The fixes in ranks 2, 9 and 20 cut the count roughly in half.

### 6.3 Something breaks at 5:40 pm CT with a 6:00 pm lock

- **The new export will not parse** (a new column, a late-added player, an encoding change). Ingest fails closed with a plain-language error. Contingency swaps cannot be applied, because they run after ingest. Fallback ladder level 5 ("the last validated file ... re-validated against the newest download", §8) needs the same parser that just failed. The code freeze blocks a fix unless the user runs `/night unlock` and debugs at T-20, which is the golf failure (RR3 item 2).
- **Claude hits its usage limit.** `/news`, `/swap` and `/status` do not run (R7: Claude Code "blocks further requests until the reset time"). The user has never run the terminal path.
- **In both cases** the engine-free options are the prebuilt "IF <player> OUT" files (rank 2), which were validated at build time against the earlier download, and the `dfs` launcher (rank 6). Neither exists in the current design. Add both, and add a one-line "if the engine is down" card to the top of the brief.
